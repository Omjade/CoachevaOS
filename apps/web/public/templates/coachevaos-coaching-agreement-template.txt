COACHING AGREEMENT

This agreement is between [Coach Name / Business Name] ("Coach") and [Client Name] ("Client"), effective [Start Date].

1. SCOPE OF COACHING
Coach will provide coaching services focused on [coaching niche/focus area, e.g. "business growth," "career transition," "health and fitness"]. Coaching is a collaborative process intended to help Client clarify goals and take action toward them. Coaching is NOT therapy, medical advice, financial advice, or legal advice, and Client should seek a licensed professional for those needs.

2. SESSION STRUCTURE
- Session frequency: [e.g. weekly / bi-weekly]
- Session length: [e.g. 45 minutes]
- Format: [video call / phone / in-person]
- Total program length: [e.g. 12 weeks, or ongoing month-to-month]

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
- Fee: [$X per session / $X per package / $X per month]
- Payment schedule: [due on booking / monthly in advance]
- Accepted payment methods: [list]
- Late payment: [grace period and consequence, e.g. sessions paused after X days overdue]

4. CANCELLATION AND RESCHEDULING
- Client may reschedule or cancel a session with at least [24/48] hours' notice at no charge.
- Sessions cancelled with less notice [are forfeited / are charged at full rate / may be rescheduled once per month at no charge] — pick the policy that matches your practice.
- Coach will provide at least [24/48] hours' notice if Coach needs to reschedule.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Coach will keep all information shared by Client confidential, except where disclosure is required by law (e.g. imminent risk of harm to self or others) or where Client gives explicit written permission to share information with a named third party.

6. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES
Client agrees to attend scheduled sessions, communicate openly, and take ownership of the actions and decisions that follow from coaching. Coaching results depend on Client's own effort and engagement, not solely on Coach's guidance.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Coach makes no guarantees of specific outcomes. Client acknowledges that coaching involves personal responsibility for actions taken, and Coach is not liable for decisions Client makes as a result of coaching conversations.

8. TERM AND TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with [X days'] written notice. Sessions already paid for but not yet delivered will be [refunded on a pro-rata basis / forfeited / credited toward future sessions] — specify your actual policy.

9. SIGNATURES

Coach: _______________________  Date: _______
Client: ______________________  Date: _______
