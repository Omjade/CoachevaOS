## G2

**Product name:** CoachevaOS
**Category:** Coaching Software / Client Management Software
**Tagline (short description, ~150 chars):**
One calm system for client records, follow-ups, bookings, billing, and AI-guided coaching. Built for independent coaches, not enterprise teams.

**Long description:**
CoachevaOS replaces the usual coach's toolkit of a spreadsheet, a messaging app, a scheduling link, and a payments tool with one connected system. Every client's intake, goals, progress, session notes, tasks, and billing status live in one record, shared with the client through their own branded portal. An AI daily briefing tells a coach who needs attention each morning instead of requiring a manual scan of every client, and a churn-risk score flags a disengaging client weeks before they'd otherwise cancel. Built for solo and small coaching practices across any niche, fitness, business, career, life, executive, and more, not just fitness.
